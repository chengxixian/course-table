# Composition Rubric

Use only dimensions relevant to the supplied artifact:

- brief/intent alignment;
- melody and motif clarity;
- harmonic function and movement;
- rhythmic/groove coherence;
- lyric fit where applicable;
- structure and development;
- memorable or purposeful contrast;
- whether repetition serves the form.

Do not reduce creative judgement to unsupported universal numeric scores. Prefer evidence-backed pass/warn/fail findings and comparative ordering when alternatives are being selected.
