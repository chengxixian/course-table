# Production Rubric

Where the available audio supports judgement, inspect:

- sound selection and sonic identity;
- layering and texture;
- performance/generation artefacts;
- production coherence across sections;
- lead intelligibility;
- excessive masking or congestion;
- transitions and local edits;
- whether the production supports the approved composition and arrangement.

Keep professional creative mixing/mastering distinct from deterministic technical QC.
