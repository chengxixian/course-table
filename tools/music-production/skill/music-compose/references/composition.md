# Composition

Treat composition as the underlying musical work, not as a DAW session or finished recording.

A composition may preserve melody, harmony, rhythm/groove, lyrics, hooks/motifs and large-scale structure. These components remain subordinate unless a real workflow gives one an independent lifecycle or handoff.

## Development order

Resolve the cheapest useful uncertainty first:

```text
idea
→ small component drafts
→ selection
→ integrated composition
→ approval
→ production handoff
```

Do not improve audio fidelity to solve a composition problem.

## Decision handling

Selection, approval and locking are different commitments.

A selected hook may still be revised. An approved melody may be relied on downstream. A locked melody should not change unless explicitly reopened.

When combining candidates, record component sources rather than pretending the result came from one winning draft.
