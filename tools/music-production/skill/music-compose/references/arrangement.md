# Arrangement

Arrangement defines how the composition is realised over time and across musical roles.

It may describe instrumentation, entries/exits, density, register, dynamics, transitions, section contrast and energy progression.

Questions such as “does verse two need more movement?” or “should the final chorus double in density?” are arrangement questions, not mastering questions.

Use section maps, MIDI, temporary instruments or rough renders before full production when those representations are sufficient.

Full-length arrangement drafts are justified when the unresolved question concerns overall pacing, duration, repetition or energy arc.
