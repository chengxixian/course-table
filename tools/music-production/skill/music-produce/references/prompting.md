# Music Production Prompting

Translate domain intent into the selected model's actual schema.

Potential musical inputs include:

- purpose and use context;
- tempo and meter;
- tonal character;
- structure and section behaviour;
- instrumentation;
- groove and energy arc;
- lyrics and vocal direction;
- production aesthetic;
- duration;
- reference material;
- properties to preserve;
- region/properties allowed to change.

Do not force every model through a universal lowest-common-denominator request object. Inspect the selected model's current inputs and express only what it supports.
